Morning Brief

Dashboard →

Personality

The standing instruction given to your local model every morning. It stays the same run to run, so this is where the briefing gets its identity and tone.

Voice

Any of Microsoft's online neural voices. If the network is down at 06:30 the briefing falls back to the offline Windows voice automatically.

Location

Sets the weather and the timezone the briefing runs on.

Music

Cider opens and plays this playlist, then fades down while the briefing is read and comes back up afterwards.

Calendar

Drop in an .ics export and the briefing will mention what's coming up — due dates, appointments, anything not on your fixed timetable.

Drop an .ics file here, or click to choose one

Verse of the day

A short passage read at the end of the briefing. Chosen from the date, so it's the same all day and changes at midnight. Quoted exactly as written — it is never passed through the local model.

Markets

One ticker per line. JSE codes end in .JO (NPN.JO), London in .L; an index starts with a caret (^GSPC); crypto looks like BTC-USD. Bare codes are US listings.

News

Untick a source to drop it. Unreachable feeds are skipped silently at run time.

Schedule & test

Changing the time re-registers the Windows scheduled task.
